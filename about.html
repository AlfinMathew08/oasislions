Dubai Oasis Lions Club

Service. Community. Purpose.

Dubai Oasis Lions Club unites civic-minded professionals and humanitarian volunteers in the United Arab Emirates under a single guiding belief: Together We Can.

As a chartered club of Lions Clubs International, we channel collective expertise and compassion into measurable community action — advancing sight conservation, hunger relief, youth mentorship, diabetes awareness, and environmental stewardship across Dubai.

Our Mission

Empowering Volunteers to Serve

To empower volunteers to serve their communities, meet humanitarian needs, encourage peace, and promote international understanding through Lions clubs.

Our Vision

Trusted Humanitarian Stewardship in Dubai

To stand as a respected community partner in the United Arab Emirates, delivering dignified, transparent, and sustainable service initiatives that uplift lives.

Global Causes

Our Core Areas of Service

In alignment with Lions Clubs International, Dubai Oasis Lions Club focuses its volunteer efforts and community partnerships across five foundational humanitarian pillars.

Vision & Sight Conservation

Supporting preventable blindness screenings, optometric checkups, and corrective eyewear outreach for community workers.

Hunger Relief & Nutrition

Organizing dignified food care distributions and balanced nutritional support packages in collaboration with licensed partners.

Youth, Health & Environment

Mentoring next-generation student leaders, raising diabetes wellness awareness, and planting native trees in UAE nature reserves.
