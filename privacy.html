Privacy Policy

Dubai Oasis Lions Club respects the privacy of our visitors, members, and community partners. This policy outlines how information is handled across our web properties.

Information Collection

We collect only necessary information explicitly submitted through our contact and volunteer enquiry forms (such as names, email addresses, and phone numbers). We do not sell or lease personal data.

Security & Storage

All submitted data is stored within an encrypted Supabase database protected by PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS) policies. Only authorized club administrators have access to submitted records.
